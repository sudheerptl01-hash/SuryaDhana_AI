## 2. Overall: 6.0 → **6.5**

- **The arithmetic.** The reconciled area mean is 6.38 (the raters' own numbers average 6.56). On 4 Nov the synthesis
  put the overall 0.3 above the area mean (mean 5.69 → overall 6.0), because the end-of-day analyst terminal was already
  very strong. Doing the same tonight gives ≈ 6.6–6.7.
- **Why I hold it at 6.5.** Tonight's review found three app-wide correctness defects that a desk would hit in its first
  hour:
  1. upcoming corporate events invisible on nine pages;
  2. the gold fair open wrong by a whole session;
  3. four volatility labels or calculations wrong on the pages the owner asked for.

  They are all cheap to fix (05), but they are what a trader sees tonight.
- **The 4 Nov framing, updated.**
  - The **end-of-day analyst's terminal** moves from ≈ 7.5 to ≈ 8.0: the IV store, drivers, data health and honest
    labels.
  - The **trading-day terminal** moves only from ≈ 5.5 to ≈ 5.75. Breeze ticks into every pane, real SPAN, Greeks and the
    OMON → OSTR handoff are real gains. But there is still no F&O book with net Greeks, no options alerts, no
    expiry-day tools, no multi-expiry structures, no phone surface, and an empty watch monitor.
  - Weighted as on 4 Nov, that is 6.5.

## 6. What did not change since 4 Nov, and should be said plainly

- The owner can still not **run a trading day** in the terminal: no F&O position book, no options alerts, no expiry-day
  view, no phone surface. Every structure priced on OSTR disappears when the tab closes.
- **Linking** stops at OMON → OSTR, and GO's OMON jump bypasses even that.
- **The appliance** still tells nobody when it is dark (no dead-man ping, no power-on restart, FileVault blocking
  unattended boots), and the 4.4 GB database has no off-machine copy.
- **The honesty engine is the moat, and it held.** Tonight's three new pre-registered studies all ended FAIL or
  PROVISIONAL, and the badges followed. The faults found are inconsistencies *between* pages and stale copies of
  verdicts, not dishonest numbers. That is what makes them cheap to fix.
