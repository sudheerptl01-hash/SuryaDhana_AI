# GS-P4 · Month-of-year seasonality of gold in rupees: findings (04 Nov 2026)

Pre-registered (`PLAN.md`); tables in `REPORT.md`; script `scripts/gold_studies/gs_p4_seasonality_in_inr.py`. Monthly log returns of gold in rupees (USD gold × USD/INR), 644 months 1973-02-28 → 2026-09-30 plus a tradable month-end sample 2000 →; month-of-year is calendar knowledge (no release lag); plain t on non-overlapping months; the walk-forward rule learns each year's month set from completed prior years only; 0.15 % per switch.

**Verdict: VOID (pre-registered statistic) / FAIL (post-hoc drift-adjusted, capped) — drift, not a calendar. The pre-registered statistic (each month's mean vs zero) is VOID by the plan's own control rule: its shuffle control passes in 44% of 100 seeds (seeds 20261027–20261126; independent probes in PLAN N4) because rupee gold drifts 1.03 %/month. Drift-adjusted (month minus the all-month mean, adopted post hoc and capped at CONTEXT-ONLY), no month reaches |t| ≥ 2 in both halves of 1973 → (nor in the full sample); the festival months do not pass; the walk-forward best-6-months rule FAILs after costs (signed: it must pay).**
