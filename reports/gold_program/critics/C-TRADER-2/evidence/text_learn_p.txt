basis
The gap between a futures price and the reference it is compared with. On this page: MCX basis = MCX front settle ÷ duty-paid landed − 1 (%); on COMEX: futures − London spot in $/oz.
Fair basis = the carry to expiry (§3.3); the residual (actual − fair) is the stress or premium gauge. Both legs must share a clock: a settlement divided by a live price is clock error, not basis.

bp = basis point = 0.01 percentage point: a yield moving 2.60 % → 2.64 % moved +4 bp. pp = percentage point: the gap between two percentages (2 % → 3 % is +1 pp, or +50 %).

fund-driver labelalso driver · driver label · fund driver · new longs · new shorts · long liquidation · funds bought the dip · funds sold into it · bought the dip · sold into it
Six words for the week's dominant fund move: new longs · short covering · funds sold into it (price up); long liquidation · new shorts · funds bought the dip (price down). The larger leg wins.
ΔL / ΔS = managed money's w/w change in long / short contracts. Down-week: long liquidation if ΔL < 0 and |ΔL| ≥ |ΔS|; new shorts if ΔS > 0 and ΔS ≥ |ΔL|; else funds bought the dip (price down, net up). Up-week, mirrored: short covering if ΔS < 0 and |ΔS| ≥ |ΔL|; new longs if ΔL > 0 and ΔL ≥ |ΔS|; else funds sold into it (price up, net down). Ties go to the leg moving with the price. 27 Oct 2026: +1,538 longs, +182 shorts, price −1.64 % → funds bought the dip. Narration of the week that ended (G-STUDY §5.2, right ≈ 3 weeks in 4); never a forecast.

supply and demand (gold)also supply · demand · above-ground stock · what is gold
Stock ≈ 216,000 t; annual mine ≈ 3,600 t (1.7% of stock) + recycling ≈ 1,200 t; demand = jewellery ~2,000 t, bars/coins ~1,200 t, central banks ~1,000 t, tech ~300 t, ± ETFs.
Gold is a stock market: price is set by who wants to hold the existing stock (investors, central banks), not by this year's mine output.

T+1also T+2 · T+3 · lag · one-day lag · publication lag
Published one business day after the day it describes: FRED yields, CME's daily OI file (≈ 15:30 IST next day), GLD tonnes. The page shows the data's date, never a clock.

z-scorealso z · z52 · z156 · zscore · standard deviations · sigma
How many standard deviations a value sits from its average over a window (z52 = the last 52 reports). |z| ≥ 2 is unusual for that window; it says nothing about next week.
z = (value − mean) ÷ standard deviation over N observations. POSITIONING shows z52 and z156 for managed-money net as a crowdedness read; G-STUDY (26 Oct 2026) found no forecasting power at 1/4/13 weeks.
