Good Delivery Current List - Gold

Only refiners whose bars have been accredited by LBMA as meeting the exacting standards for trading on the global OTC market appear in the Good Delivery List.

The Good Delivery List is the property of LBMA. Use of the Good Delivery List or any proprietary information contained therein as part of an accreditation procedure is subject to license by LBMA. For further information in relation to our licence agreement, please contact [email protected]. You can also view refiners who are now on the former Good Delivery List.
