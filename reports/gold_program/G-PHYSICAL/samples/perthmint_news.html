Fast facts

Operating since 1899, The Perth Mint is renowned globally for its minting excellence and as a must-see place to visit in Western Australia

Manufacturing

We have capacity to process more than 800 tonnes of gold and 1000 tonnes of silver in our refinery annually and are one of Australia’s largest manufacturers.

Exporting

As one of Western Australia’s top exporters, our annual turnover was AUD  44.23 billion in 2025-26.

Tourism

One of Perth’s premier tourist attractions, 97,192 people enjoyed our gold tour and more than 316,000 people visited our multi award-winning heritage premises in 2025-26.

Coins

We sold more than 9.45 million coins, medallions and minted bars in 2025-26.
