Which algorithm earns the 5-year run?

Thirteen candidates, one fixed protocol: purged walk-forward on ~2,100 NSE tickers, four monthly CV folds (Apr–Jul 2026) plus one untouched holdout. Primary task beat_mkt_20d — will a stock beat the universe median over the next 20 sessions. The question was which algorithm, not whether the edge is already tradable.

Verdict · 2026-10-15

Carry LightGBM on the 10-feature selected subset to the 5-year run, with L2 logistic regression retained as the interpretable benchmark. Drop RL and the RNNs.

02 · The regime problem

CV and the holdout rank models in near-opposite order

12 of 13 candidates scored better on the single Aug→mid-Sep holdout than on CV (mean +3.2 AUC pts), and the two views invert the ranking (Spearman −0.60). The paired bootstrap says both directions are “significant” under date-resampling — so the disagreement is regime-window dependence, not sampling noise. April was adversarial for everyone; the holdout was friendly to everyone.

Who is actually above chance?

Pooled-CV AUC with 95% CI; open dashed markers are the clean re-validations. Blue = CI clear of 0.50; red = significantly below (the 1-feature momentum rank was mildly contrarian in this sample); gray = not distinguishable from coin-flipping — which includes full-feature XGB/LGBM (P=0.61/0.65), MLP, LSTM, RL and the frozen lr_l2 (P=0.90). On the holdout, every model clears 0.5 at P=1.000 — the friendly-window effect in one line.

Paired champion-minus-rival deltas: ● CV mean vs ○ holdout. Champion vs lr_l2: +0.0181 on CV (CI [0.0111, 0.0256]) but a dead heat on the holdout (−0.0008, P=0.46) — with only 4 regime-months the bootstrap cannot capture fold-level regime risk, so the CV gap is suggestive, not settled. Against XGB the sign flips outright (+0.0284 CV, −0.0426 holdout). Individual CIs are ±0.009–0.012 wide: the mid-table ordering is noise — do not read order into ~0.01–0.02 differences.

The NSE delivery block

NSE publishes, per stock and session, the share of traded volume actually taken to delivery rather than squared off intraday — a conviction signal most price-only pipelines never see. Two delivery features (deliv_pct_sma_20, deliv_pct) made the subset on merit: the dataset's main non-price information.

Redundancy

18 pairs at |ρ|>0.85, 9 clusters at |ρ|>0.9 (cs_rank twins, SMA-distance/RSI family, mom_12_1↔ret_252d ρ=0.93) — fine for trees, destabilizing for linear coefficients; one representative each enters the subset.

Full-model views agree: LGBM gain top-5 — atr_pct, deliv_pct_sma_20, cs_rank_ret_126, cs_rank_vol_20, ret_126d; lr_l2 per-1σ top coefficients — rsi_14 (+0.25), cs_rank_turnover (+0.19), dist_sma_20 (-0.16), deliv_pct_sma_20 (+0.15), macd_norm (-0.13).

rl_reinforce — a bandit that loses to a free heuristic

CV AUC 0.5085 ± 0.022; on 2 of 4 folds REINFORCE never beat its own untrained initialization on inner-validation reward. 09 · Methodology

Protocol — fixed, shared, no per-model deviation

Splits

Purged walk-forward, 22-session embargo (covers the 20-session label overlap). Folds = test months 2026-04…07 (88 test dates); holdout 2026-08-03 → 09-11/15 (32 dates), used once, never for tuning.

Tuning

Hyper-parameters tuned on the first two CV folds (2026-04/05) or inner time-ordered splits of train only; identical preprocessing harness; seed 108 everywhere.

Universe & label

Point-in-time filter: close ≥ ₹10, trailing-20d median turnover ≥ ₹1 crore (no survivorship bias). beat_mkt_20d entered at next open; y_clean_20d masks split/suspension-poisoned windows (7.2% of rows).

Inference

Date-block bootstrap on saved predictions: test dates resampled within folds, B=1000, models paired on identical draws. Headline metric ROC AUC; economics on score deciles of y_excess_20d.

Limitations

  • Six months ≈ 4 regime-months. The bootstrap conditions on those months and cannot capture fold-level regime risk — the dominant risk this sample exposed. The champion-vs-linear CV gap is suggestive, not settled.
  • Prices are unadjusted (no corporate-action feed): the y_clean_20d mask is the only defense; never evaluate on masked rows.
  • No carry or announcement features — they need F&O/announcement feeds this repo doesn't ingest.
  • 2026 archive provenance. The 2026 portion trades every weekday (no holiday closures — a simulated continuous calendar), and the holiday-echo incident came from the same archive mirror: build no calendar/seasonality features on 2026 data.
  • up_5d label defect (NaN forward returns hard-labeled 0, ~1.6% of rows) is fixed in the cleaned rebuild; the fix applies to future builds — treat every frozen up_5d number as a sanity check only.
  • Long-lookback features are mostly imputed early on: ret_252d / mom_12_1 / dist_200dma are 58 / 58 / 21% NaN in the pilot and "switch on" through 2026.
