Do the features need a rebuild before the 5-year run?

Before scaling the pilot to five years of data, we checked whether its features need rebuilding or fine-tuning. An exploratory analysis measured how each feature relates to the next 20-day excess return, month by month, across 469,152 labelled rows and 318 trading dates. Then 23 new features were built without look-ahead and tested against the pilot champion in the same walk-forward harness, with the pass mark fixed before any result was seen.

Verdict · 15 Oct 2026

No rebuild now. Keep LightGBM on the 10 selected features. All five engineered variants scored below it. The analysis does change how the 5-year run should select and monitor features, and the list of changes is at the end of this page.

The new features were strong one at a time. The best, cs_rank_deliv_sma, had a cross-sectional IC of +0.081 at build time. Added to the model, they made it worse. The likely cause is redundancy, since 4 of the new features correlate 0.96 to 0.99 with features the model already uses. The trees then split on near-copies and spread a fixed 300-tree budget thinner. The four market-state features did the most damage on the holdout (0.4997). Four CV months hold too few regime changes for a tree model to learn when those features matter. A logistic model on the rank features alone reached 0.5068, below the pilot's logistic benchmark of 0.5171. These results come from a 6-month sample, so each idea is queued for a retest on five years of data.

What the analysis found

March 2026 changed which features work

IC here is the rank correlation between a feature and the next 20-day excess return across all stocks on a date, averaged over each month. In March 2026, after a sell-off that left the median stock down 8.6% over 20 sessions, 25 of the 40 base features had their worst month at once. The pilot's first CV fold was trained on data up to 25 February 2026 and tested in April, so it learned the old relationships and was scored on the new ones. That is why April was the hardest fold for every model.

Most stable

Delivery is the most consistent signal, and it is fading

deliv_pct had a positive IC in 15 of 16 months (t = 4.35). It is the only feature whose average IC is larger than its month-to-month swing. deliv_pct_sma_20 held up on rising and falling market days alike (+0.106 and +0.059). After the break both shrank: deliv_pct from +0.107 to +0.020 a month on average, and deliv_pct_sma_20 from +0.128 to +0.009. Delivery is the champion's main non-price input, so production needs an alert on its rolling IC.

Conditional

Momentum pays when the market is rising

mom_12_1 averaged an IC of +0.099 on days when the median stock was up over the past 20 sessions and +0.013 on the other days (t = 4.11 for the gap). Its deciles are flat through the seventh, and the effect sits in the top three. Momentum is also stronger among volatile stocks. Moving from low to high momentum adds +2.20 points of 20-day excess return when volatility is high and +1.14 points when it is low, in the same direction in 6 of 7 months.

Unstable

The volatility features change sign with the regime

atr_pct averaged −0.147 over the nine months to February 2026 and +0.091 from March. vol_20 moved from −0.130 to +0.056. The pilot's CV folds (April to July) saw only the positive phase, so the selection study gave these features a positive sign that a full cycle will not support. Market-state inputs such as the market's drawdown or the days since a sell-off might let a model learn when the sign flips. Testing that needs many regimes, so it waits for the 5-year data.

Real, but confounded

Recently listed stocks earn a premium

Within the same month, stocks with less than a year of history beat seasoned stocks by +1.24 points a month on average (positive in 5 of 7 months, t = 2.25). Pooled across all months the gap reverses to −0.51 points, because the pilot database starts in March 2025 and every stock looks new before March 2026. The pilot's proxy, a missing 252-day return, mixes the real effect with that artifact. The 5-year build should use real listing dates.

Delivery stays positive in almost every month. The volatility family changes sign at the break. In the two holdout months the long-momentum and 52-week-high features averaged an IC of +0.16, against +0.03 in the four CV months. That friendly window is why nearly every model in the pilot scored better on the holdout than in CV. These short-horizon features have |t| below 1 across 16 months and flip sign from month to month: mom_10, mom_5, dist_sma_20, dist_sma_10, dist_sma_5, rsi_2, ret_1d, gap_pct, close_pos_in_range, vol_z_20. The champion's cs_rank_mom_20 does no better (t = 0.42) and is the first candidate to drop when selection is re-run.

Shapes

How returns change across each feature's deciles

Mean 20-day excess return by decile, pooled over 16 months, for the champion's features. ret_126d and cs_rank_ret_126 have identical deciles and share a chart. Each label gives the analysis' shape call, the top-minus-bottom spread and the number of months in which that spread was positive.

Delivery and 52-week-high rise steadily. The long-momentum features are convex, with most of their effect in the top two or three deciles. atr_pct peaks in decile 8 and falls back. cs_rank_vol_20 climbs to the ninth decile and then drops in the tenth (+0.7% against +1.8%), so the most volatile tenth of stocks underperforms. Trees pick up these shapes on their own. A linear model needs them spelled out, for example as a flag for the top volatility decile.

Capacity

The signal does not depend on illiquid stocks

Monthly IC within each third of the universe by turnover, assigned per date. The composite averages the cross-sectional ranks of the champion's 10 features, with signs aligned. Next to each bar is the number of months in which that IC was positive.

The composite is strongest in the middle third and still 0.058 in the most liquid third, although it holds its sign there in only 10 of 16 months. Delivery weakens as liquidity rises but keeps about two thirds of its IC (0.101 to 0.065). The spread of outcomes is the same in every third, with mean absolute excess return between 7.1% and 7.5%. Moving toward larger, more liquid names should keep most of the edge.

For the 5-year run

Changes to the scale-up plan

  • Re-run feature selection on the full history before freezing the feature set, starting from the pilot's 10.
  • Report results by regime. Label each month by market state, keep the worst-fold floor as a pass condition, and expect one or two months per cycle like March 2026.
  • Run three separate A/Bs against the re-selected baseline: market-state inputs, the momentum and volatility product, and listing age. Fix each pass mark before running it.
  • Monitor delivery in production. Track the rolling IC of the delivery features and the drift of every input.
  • Keep the date-block significance test. Daily ICs are close to fully dependent (lag-1 autocorrelation 0.97), so they are not independent samples.
