01 · Verdict board

Verdict in one page

Signal

Over 43 monthly out-of-sample folds (January 2023 to July 2026), a LightGBM classifier on all end-of-day features ranks stocks by 20-session relative performance with mean AUC 0.5366, above 0.50 in 81% of months, worst month 0.453 (March 2026). The honest nested-selection variant scores 0.5359 (20-session block-bootstrap 95% interval [0.5250, 0.5483]; per-date rank IC +0.065, Newey-West t 6.0, positive in 38 of 43 months). A one-feature momentum rule scores 0.4951. The pilot's 10-feature subset, chosen on 2026 data, scores 0.5251 at 5 years (−0.0115 [−0.0159, −0.0071] against all features): its selection did not transfer.

Source: ml/results/scale5y/*.json

Size

Per-date rank information coefficient (IC) is 0.04 to 0.05 on the pilot and 0.065 over the five years. That is the size the literature finds at the frontier: GKX's neural nets imply a forecast-return correlation of about 0.06 on US data, Qlib's LightGBM reaches IC 0.045 on CSI300.

Source: ml/research/SOTA_FORECASTING.md, ml/results/label_study/model_level.json

Economics

Over 913 out-of-sample signal dates (January 2023 to September 2026), net of 20 bps per side, no single machine-learned model's top decile beats simply holding the universe equally weighted (+19.4% a year): the LightGBM books trail it by 0.7 to 1.2 points a year and beat it in 20 of 45 months. The pre-registered three-model rank ensemble is the one exception: +7.0% a year over the universe, information ratio 1.26 with a 95% interval of [0.32, 2.37], positive in every calendar year. Its Deflated Sharpe Ratio at an honestly counted 177 trials is 0.16 (0.04 for the liquid book pre-registered as the paper portfolio), so it does not survive multiple-testing adjustment.

Source: ml/results/scale5y/portfolio_5y.json, ml/results/scale5y/SCALE5Y.md

Pre-registered promotion gates

LightGBM with nested selection passes 0 of 5, LightGBM on all features 0 of 5, the rank ensemble 1 of 5. Gate 1 (beats L2 logistic on mean CV AUC with an interval excluding zero) fails for all three: the logistic model's mean is higher (delta −0.0056 [−0.0150, +0.0024] for nested LightGBM). Gate 2 (fold floor ≥ 0.50 in ≥ 90% of months): 86%, 81%, 91% (the ensemble's one pass). Gate 3 (top decile beats the equal-weight universe in ≥ 75% of months net of 20 bps): 44%, 44%, 73%. Gate 4 (selected-feature overlap ≥ 7 of 10 across yearly re-selections): 4, 4, 5. Gate 5 (DSR ≥ 0.95 on the liquid top decile, N = 177): 0.000, 0.000, 0.040. The pre-registered criteria for committing capital are not met by any model.

Source: ml/SCALE_UP.md, ml/results/scale5y/scale5y_summary.json

Integrity

The pipeline is clean: labels and features recompute exactly from raw data, point-in-time universe verified, label-permutation null 0.500 ± 0.001, no duplicate sessions in 1,245 trading days. The pilot's headline (CV AUC 0.531, “only model above chance”) was inflated by feature selection on test months and has been corrected in ml/MODEL_SELECTION.md.

Source: ml/results/audit/audit.json

The three deliverables

Feature set

The 52 end-of-day candidates: the 50 base features plus deliv_value_share_20 and amihud_20, the two that honest yearly re-selection keeps picking. The single-model reference feeds all 52 to LightGBM without hand selection; the ensemble as tested re-selects 5 to 15 of them each year, which buys nothing on AUC (−0.0007 [−0.0052, +0.0038]). The pilot's 10-feature subset is retired. No further feature engineering on bhavcopy-derived inputs; the next gains require external data (corporate actions first).

Evidence: Features, Five-year walk-forward

Label set

Primary label_beat_mkt_20d (20-session next-open excess vs the cross-sectional median, clean-masked). Economics judged against the equal-weight universe mean, rank-IC and net spread reported beside AUC, ETFs excluded, 40-day label carried as a candidate, up_5d demoted to a negative control.

Evidence: Labels

Model

For paper trading only: the pre-registered rank-average ensemble (LightGBM champion config + LambdaRank + CatBoost with fixed tree budgets, per-date rank average), frozen as one strategy. Single-model reference: LightGBM, fixed 300 trees, all features, rolling 504-session window, monthly retrain, 22-session embargo, exit-date purge. L2 logistic (C = 0.01) kept as the drift monitor, not a scorer. Deep sequence models, transformers, DLinear, time-series foundation models and reinforcement learning are dropped with evidence. Nothing is promoted to capital.

02 · Status board

What works, what does not, what is unknown

Working

  • Data pipeline: 5 years ingested, resilient to archive quirks, no duplicate sessions, resume-safe, 19 of 19 tests.
  • Evaluation harness: purged walk-forward, nested selection, exit-date purging, block bootstraps, pre-registered pass marks, one result schema that every team used.
  • The signal: delivery and long momentum, AUC 0.53 to 0.54 over 43 months, above 0.50 in 81% to 86% of months, consistent with published frontier numbers.
  • Model choice: LightGBM with fixed trees on all features; it is the most stable thing in the league and nothing from deep learning, ranking losses or attention beats it.
  • Honesty: the one inflated claim was found by the program's own red team and corrected before any capital decision.
  • The ensemble's five-year economics: +7.0% a year over the universe with an interval clear of zero, positive in every year; the only book in the program with that property.
  • The deliberate neutral construction (§13.2): a LightGBM score residualised per date on volatility and size turns its top decile from trailing the universe into beating it, +6.9% (nested) and +6.5% (all features) a year net of 20 bps, both IR intervals clear of zero (B1 passes); +7.8% a year for the control on the corrected data and +10.5% over the ten-year record.
  • Training history (§14.2): the same LightGBM on an expanding window raises the ten-year CV AUC from 0.5455 to 0.5534 (+0.0079 [+0.0047, +0.0113]) and the rank IC from 0.088 to 0.103; its neutral books gain +4.44 (full universe) and +3.54 (liquid) points a year, seed-averaged, with intervals clear of zero; the first configuration in the program to pass gate 1. Exploratory and concentrated in falling markets; in the forward record as a challenger, not promoted.

Not working

  • Tradable edge: not demonstrated. No single model's long-only book beats the equal-weight universe net of costs over five years; the ensemble's does, with DSR 0.16 (0.04 for the paper book) at 177 trials.
  • Pre-registered gates: nested LightGBM 0 of 5, all-features LightGBM 0 of 5, ensemble 1 of 5.
  • Machine learning over a linear model: not shown; the logistic model's mean AUC is higher and every paired interval includes zero.i §14.3the expanding window beats the logistic benchmark on every seed (+0.0112 [+0.0049, +0.0179] for seed 108), against a logistic model on the rolling window: evidence, not a goal; an expanding-window logistic model is untested.
  • Feature selection: unstable year to year; buys nothing on AUC.
  • Extreme-decile economics: the top decile holds a lottery tail in down markets; book construction must change (liquidity filter, equal-weight benchmark, possibly top quintile or rank-weighted rather than top decile).
  • The linear model as a scorer: highest mean, worst tails; demoted to drift monitor.
  • Deep learning, transformers, foundation models, reinforcement learning: nothing beats trees here; stopped.
  • Feature and model A/Bs (§13.4): none of the eight A/Bs passes its pre-registered mark (at least +0.003 AUC with an interval clear of zero), and neither do AB0 and the sector placebo; the real gains (52 vs 50 features +0.0014, F&O +0.0020) are below the bar.
  • The liquid, capacity-bearing book over ten years (§13.6): the control's neutral liquid-tercile book earns +1.2% a year, IR 0.21 [−0.52, 0.93], ahead in 55 of 105 months.
  • Phase 2 levers (§14.1): a liquid-only model, a futures-hedged long-short, a 40-day horizon, announcement-event features and hyper-parameter tuning each fail the pre-registered mark; none should be retried without new information.

Pending

  • No research run is pending: every run in the program is complete and committed. (The label study closed with a clean end-to-end rerun that reproduced all 13 of its JSON outputs with zero numeric differences.)
  • The forward record (§14.4): six books since 2026-10-19; the first tranche exits on 2026-11-18, and the live comparison between the frozen book and the expanding-window challenger is read under a rule registered before any live month matured: descriptive after 2027-04-19, a decision after 2027-10-19, an interval test after 2028-10-19 (ml/paper/SPEC.md section 6).

Unknown (cannot be decided with this data)

  • Whether the ensemble's +7% a year is skill or a favourable factor mix over 45 months of one, mostly rising, market.i §13.2a deliberate volatility+size-neutral construction gives single LightGBM models the same kind of excess; it was read from the same folds, so it is a consistency check, not proof.
  • Whether delivery % is a durable signal or a 2025 artefact: its IC fell by two-thirds after March 2026; it needs the 5-year by-year test and, ideally, independent literature that does not exist.i §13.3the residual IC is durable (3 of 4 and 4 of 4 years, both liquidity halves); the raw IC is not.
  • Anything that needs several market cycles: market-state conditioning, seasonality, sector rotation.i §13.6over 103 folds from 2018 to 2026 the control's mean fold AUC is above 0.50 in every calendar year and the “not one regime” reading is met (0.5434); market-state inputs still add nothing (AB1).i §14.2the expanding window's AUC gain sits mostly in falling markets (+0.024 in 25 down months against +0.004 in 55 up months): a model that remembers earlier sell-offs does better in the next one.
  • Realistic costs in the illiquid tail: name-level spread and impact are not modelled.i §13.2a pessimistic name-level model (Corwin-Schultz spread plus square-root impact) charges about 45 bps a side on the neutral books.

Labels: which target to model

Source: ml/results/label_study/LABEL_STUDY.md (28 label variants: 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20 and 40 sessions; close-to-close and next-open entry; raw, excess, rank and vol-scaled targets; 40 features; 1,106 testable hypotheses).

Left: predictability by horizon from label_summary.json (Benjamini-Hochberg q < 0.05 with Newey-West standard errors; without the overlap correction the 40-session counts are 6 and 5). Middle and right: the champion LightGBM retrained on each label (model_level.json); the excess label was run at 2 to 20 sessions and the raw label at 5 and 20.

Findings

  1. Predictability falls with horizon. 21 to 22 of 40 features pass Benjamini-Hochberg q < 0.05 at 1 day, 4 to 5 at 20 days, none at 40 days once overlapping windows are corrected for. Selecting features on the first 8 months and scoring the rest gives an out-of-sample IC of +0.05 at 1 to 2 days (t 2.8 to 4.2), +0.03 at 5 days (t ≈ 1), and about zero from 10 to 20 days, because of the March-2026 regime break. Delivery is the one pillar that ranks in the top five at every horizon and for both entry conventions (t 4.5 at 20 days even with ETFs excluded); at 40 days the single best feature is dist_52w_high (IC 0.10).
  2. The excess label is the most predictable by a model, at any horizon. Model rank-IC is flat at about 0.044 from 3 to 20 days; CV AUC sits between 0.518 and 0.524 at every horizon. The raw > 0 labels post the highest CV AUC (0.559) but that is market timing, not stock selection: their fold base rates swing from 0.44 to 0.72, their holdout AUC in a down market is 0.47 to 0.51 and their cross-sectional rank-IC is a quarter to two-fifths of the excess label's.
  3. Only the 20-day label clears realistic costs. Its long-short decile spread breaks even at 26.7 bps per side at 100% turnover and nets +3.4% to +5.6% a year (at 100% and at measured turnover), with a gross spread t of only 1.28 over 6 out-of-sample months; horizons of 5 days or less lose 8% to 61% a year after costs. Long-only, every horizon is negative after costs against the equal-weight universe mean, because the top decile holds a lottery tail that does worse than deciles 7 to 9.
  4. The universe median is a soft benchmark. Right skew puts the equal-weight universe's own “excess over the median” at +1.4% to +2.0% per 20 days (about 22% a year in the strategy study). Measured against the universe mean, the pilot champion's top-decile excess was positive in 3 of 5 windows, not 5 of 5.
  5. Multiple testing. 1,106 hypotheses; 407 nominal p < 0.05 and 297 BH discoveries observed. Under a dependence-preserving month-sign-flip null the 95th percentile of nominal hits is 229, so the grid is significant at about p = 0.005. But the evidence is effectively 17 monthly draws, and the selection test shows it does not carry forward beyond about 5 days.

Decision

Keep label_beat_mkt_20d (20-session next-open excess vs the cross-sectional median, clean-masked) as the primary label. Shorter labels are statistically easier but costs dominate them; next-open entry is realistic and costs nothing in predictability; raw, excess and rank targets give identical per-date ICs so the cross-sectional target choice does not matter; vol-scaling adds nothing. Four changes:

  • (a) judge economics against the equal-weight universe mean, not the median;
  • (b) report rank-IC and net spread next to AUC in every league table;
  • (c) carry a 40-day label as a candidate horizon for the 5-year run (needs an embargo of at least 41 sessions; untestable on the pilot);
  • (d) drop the 137 ETFs (6.1% of rows) from the stock universe.

The exact column names, definitions, clean masks, embargo lengths, the ETF exclusion rule and the reporting formulas for rank-IC, net spread and the equal-weight benchmark are in LABEL_STUDY.md, section “Labels to carry to the 5-year run”; that section is the label specification for the production team.

A negative control, not a label

up_5d is not a stock-selection label: regime-exposed (monthly base rate 0.37 to 0.72), close-to-close entry, 730 split-poisoned rows because it is unmasked, holdout AUC 0.507, rank-IC 0.010. It stays only as a negative control: the gap between its pooled and cross-sectional AUC measures how much “skill” is market timing.

Verdict on sufficiency

The end-of-day bhavcopy has been mined to its resolvable limit at this sample size. Two liquidity and delivery re-expressions carry information the 10-feature champion lacked and earn a place in the candidate set; nothing else does. Feature selection itself is unstable (gate 4 fails) and, at 5 years, buys nothing over giving LightGBM every feature (0.5359 nested vs 0.5366 all features, paired delta −0.0007 [−0.0052, +0.0038], 18 of 43 fold wins: a tie). The candidate set is therefore the 52 end-of-day features; the single-model reference uses all of them without hand selection, and the ensemble as tested re-selects yearly from the same 52 (an all-52 ensemble A/B is queued). Further engineering on these inputs is stopped. The remaining feature upside is external data, ranked in Recommendations.

What the pilot got wrong about features, and how it was found

The pilot's 10 features were ranked by univariate AUC on the four CV test months and by permutation importance on the June test month. The red team's honest re-run (AUDIT.md, T11) scored 0.5132 instead of 0.5307, level with random 10-feature subsets (0.513 ± 0.006). At 5 years the same subset scores 0.5251 and loses to all features in 32 of 43 folds. The mechanism is visible in the yearly re-selections: the pilot's set is the 2025/2026 regime's set (overlap 8 of 10 with the 2025 pick, 1 of 10 with 2024's).

Four findings from the 5-year run

  1. Feature selection did not transfer, and is not needed. The pilot's 10 features lose to all features by 0.0115 AUC [0.0071, 0.0159] and in 32 of 43 months; they average 0.4979 in 2023. Honest yearly re-selection recovers the full-feature score exactly (0.5359 vs 0.5366) at the price of unstable, alternating sets, and it helped only in 2023 (0.5170 vs 0.5092, with small training sets) and 2025 (0.5565 vs 0.5524). LightGBM's own regularisation does the job. The single-model reference uses all 52 features.
  2. The linear model has the highest mean and the worst risk. L2 logistic scores 0.5415 (0.5430 nested), above LightGBM, so pre-registered gate 1 fails as written. It is the purest defensive-factor ranker in the league: per-date score correlation −0.50 with atr_pct, −0.45 with Amihud illiquidity and +0.49 with the 52-week high (LightGBM: −0.30, −0.29, +0.25). AUC rewards that tilt in down months (0.607) and punishes it in rebounds (0.379 in March 2026); its month-to-month spread is 1.4× LightGBM's, its worst month is 0.37 to 0.38, and its holdout decile spread is −3.6%, with its bottom decile its best decile. Nonlinearity here buys stability and sane tails, not a higher mean AUC, which matches the literature's modest GBM-over-linear gains (GKX: 0.33 to 0.40% vs 0.11 to 0.27% monthly R²).
  3. AUC and tradability disagree. AUC 0.536 with an IC t of 6 coexists with no long-only edge (five-year portfolio). The label “beat the universe median” rewards low-volatility names, which cross the median often but do not beat the mean of a right-skewed, rising universe, so the classifiers' top deciles are defensive: nested LightGBM's trails the equal-weight universe by 0.58% per 20 days in up months, net of costs. The negative holdout decile spreads (every classifier −0.4% to −3.6%, the momentum rule +2.95%) are the same tilt over one 32-day window; across the 43 folds the classifiers' mean decile spreads are positive (LightGBM: +1.25% to +1.32%, positive in 30 to 33 months). The ensemble shows the fix: adding the high-volatility LambdaRank makes the blended score volatility-neutral (correlation with atr_pct −0.05), costs 0.003 of AUC and turns −0.7% a year into +7.0% a year over the universe. AUC is necessary, not sufficient; models are judged on net excess over the equal-weight universe.
  4. Hyperparameters are a mild contamination of 2026. The LightGBM configuration (num_leaves 63, learning rate 0.05, 300 trees, feature and bagging fraction 0.8, min_data_in_leaf 200) was chosen in the pilot, which saw April to July 2026. Those 4 folds and the holdout are therefore not fully out-of-sample for the configuration; the 39 folds from 2023-01 to 2026-03 are. Removing those folds leaves the ranking unchanged (39 folds, 2023-01 to 2026-03: all features 0.5376, nested 0.5364, pilot 10 features 0.5240, L2 logistic 0.5441, momentum rule 0.4956; derived from the per-fold JSONs).

What this program does not claim

  • It does not claim that machine learning beats a linear model on this problem; the logistic benchmark's mean AUC is higher and every paired interval includes zero.
  • It does not claim that the ensemble's +7% a year over the universe is skill rather than a favourable volatility-factor mix over 45 months of one, mostly rising, market.
  • It does not claim that any model is reliable month to month: the single-model candidates fall below 0.50 in 6 to 9 of 43 months, and every candidate did in March 2026.
  • It does not claim the delivery signal is durable. Its IC fell by two-thirds after one regime break and there is no external literature.
  • It does not claim the 2023 fold results are representative: those models trained on 2 to 13 months of data.
  • It does not claim the 2026 folds are fully out-of-sample for the LightGBM configuration, which was tuned on them in the pilot.
  • It does not claim that AUC differences of 0.01 between models are decidable on these samples: the paired block-bootstrap intervals are about ±0.004 to ±0.014 wide.
  • It does not claim realistic costs for the illiquid tail or for any short book.
  • It does not claim that the volatility+size-neutral books of §13.2 are independent evidence: the construction was read from the same 43 folds it is tested on; only the forward record can confirm it.
