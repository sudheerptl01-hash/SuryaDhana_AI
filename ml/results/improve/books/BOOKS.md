## 4. What the result means

**The classifiers' signal was there; their baskets hid it.** Neutralising the score against volatility and size turns nested_lgbm's top decile from -0.7% to +6.9% a year over the equal-weight universe net of 20 bps (IR +1.92 [+0.99, +3.01]) and all51's from -1.2% to +6.5% (IR +1.85 [+0.93, +2.93]): B1 passes. The re-ranked score keeps most of the ranking (per-date Spearman 0.92 with the raw score); what goes is the tilt that §6.3 finding 3 of the program report described (correlation with atr_pct -0.31 -> -0.03, with log_turnover +0.22 -> +0.02). The neutral book earns in up and in down months (+0.66% / +0.36% per 20 sessions, against -0.58% / +0.89% for the raw book) and in every calendar year (2023: +0.29%, 2024: +0.45%, 2025: +0.40%, 2026: +1.27% per 20 sessions).

**Volatility does most of the work, size sharpens it, the industry step does not help.** Volatility alone gives nested_lgbm +7.3% (IR +1.64); adding size leaves the excess about the same and raises the IR to +1.92; adding the industry step cuts it to +3.7% (IR +0.91). Only index members have an NSE industry (47% of rows), so that step is partly an index-membership adjustment and is not a clean test of industry neutrality.

**It is the ensemble's mechanism, built on purpose.** The single-model neutral books (+6.9% and +6.5%) come close to the rank ensemble's accidental +7.0% a year; the ensemble neutralised the same way earns +10.5% (IR +2.74 [+1.60, +3.90], 38/45 months ahead of the universe), the best of the 66 books.

**Costs bite harder on the neutral books.** Removing the size tilt moves the basket into smaller names (median name ADV20 Rs 28.8 crore -> 7.0 crore; names under Rs 5 crore a day 27% -> 43%). Flat break-even is 56 bps per side for nested_lgbm and 53 for all51, against 20 bps assumed. The pre-specified name-level model charges these books about 45 bps per side on the traded weight (36 bps Corwin-Schultz half-spread, 8.5 bps impact), above the 40 bps stress level, and leaves +2.0% (nested_lgbm) and +1.5% (all51) a year. That model is pessimistic (section 8).

**Turnover levers: real but smaller than the pass marks.** The buffered decile cuts turnover per rebalance by 18% to 26% (B2 needs 30%: FAIL), and it never costs net excess: higher in 11 of 12 pairs, CI entirely below zero in 0. Under the name-level costs it is the better basket (nested_lgbm vol+size +3.4% against +2.0% for the plain decile). Holding 40 sessions keeps the excess close (-0.2 pp, -0.6 pp, -1.0 pp for nested_lgbm, all51, ensemble; 2 of 3 within 1 pp) but trades 0.54 to 0.55 times as much per year, not half (B3: FAIL); read per rebalance the 40-session tranche replaces more of its basket (82% against 76%), so B3 fails under either reading. Under name-level costs the 40-session book is the best single-model construction (+4.0% a year, break-even 83 bps per side).

**No book clears the multiple-testing bar.** With the 42 new books in the trial set the cross-book variance of the daily Sharpe rises to 0.0039 (the five-year run's 24 books: 0.0017), so the expected best null Sharpe is 2.79 a year at N = 238 (2.69 at N = 177). The B1 books' DSR at N = 238 is 0.053 (nested_lgbm) and 0.038 (all51); the best book's is 0.469. Gate 3 (months ahead of the universe >= 75%): nested_lgbm 33/45 FAIL, all51 34/45 PASS. Gate 5 (DSR of the liquid book >= 0.95): 0.0001 and 0.0002, FAIL; the liquid neutral books earn +5.0% and +5.2% over the liquid universe with IR CIs [-0.14, +1.93] and [-0.09, +2.03].

**What changes.** Plan section 4 asked for a single-model book passing B1 and then gates 3 and 5 at N = 238. B1 passed; gate 3 failed for nested_lgbm (33/45); passed for all51 (34/45); gate 5 failed for both by a wide margin. Nothing is promoted and the verdict stays: no capital, the paper book decides. What this stream does establish is the mechanism: the ensemble's economics come from removing the volatility tilt, which can be done deliberately on any of the scores; and under the name-level costs the buffer and the 40-session hold add 1.1 to 2.0 points a year to the vol+size top decile. The idea was formed on these same 43 folds (finding 3 was read from them), so the B1 interval is a consistency check, not an independent confirmation; only a frozen forward record can be that.

## 8. Limits

- **The hypothesis came from these months.** Neutralisation was proposed because the ensemble, volatility-neutral by accident, worked on the same 43 folds. The grid and pass marks were fixed before any number of this stream existed, and the DSR charges for the trials, but not for where the idea came from.
- **The deflation bar depends on V[SR] as much as on N.** Pre-registered statistic: V[SR] across all 66 books (0.0039). A sensitivity that is *not* pre-registered and decides nothing: with the five-year run's V[SR] (0.0017) the best book's DSR at N = 238 would be 0.949, nested_lgbm's vol+size top decile 0.555, and the three liquid books' at most 0.256, so gate 5 would fail either way.
- **The name-level cost model is pessimistic.** Corwin-Schultz on daily high-low ranges attributes part of intraday volatility to the spread: it puts the half-spread of RELIANCE at 14 bps, HDFCBANK at 12 bps, INFY at 12 bps, TCS at 16 bps (median ADV20 Rs 1,514, 2,428, 1,031, 790 crore), where quoted spreads are a few bps at most; 33% of two-day estimates are negative before the median. The order size follows the brief (q = Rs 100 crore / basket size); one day's order in the 20-tranche program is 1/20 of that, so the impact term (8.5 of the 45 bps for nested_lgbm's vol+size decile) is overstated by about 4.5x. Read the name-level column as a stress case (for the neutral deciles it charges more than the 40 bps column), not as a central estimate.
- **Industry map**: current constituents of four NSE index lists (755 symbols), applied to every date (membership look-ahead); 53% of rows sit in one 'Unclassified' group.
- **40-session books** have no label to pin to and use unadjusted prices. The screen that keeps them clean removed 0.52% of rows whose mean 40-session return was -58% (split and bonus artefacts); like y_clean_20d it looks forward. Their signal dates end 2026-08-18 (893 dates); B3's same-dates comparison is in books.json (pass_marks.B3.scorers.*.robustness_same_signal_dates).
- **The universe filter y_clean_20d looks forward** (it drops names whose next 21 sessions contain a move above 22%); it is the program's convention, shared by every book and by the equal-weight benchmark.
- **Capacity**: the full-universe volatility-neutral deciles carry about Rs 195 to 296 crore at 5% of ADV for the 10th-percentile entering name; the liquid buffered books Rs 871 to 1,001 crore.
- **Recent months weigh heavily**: nested_lgbm's vol+size excess is +1.27% per 20 sessions in 2026 against +0.29% to +0.45% in 2023 to 2025. The 32-date holdout gives the vol+size books IRs of +3.7 to +5.9, one six-week window, not evidence.
- **Long-short books** carry no borrow cost; NSE shorts need SLB or stock futures (about 200 names).
- **Aggregation of B2 and B3 across scorers** (every pair / every scorer must pass) was fixed before results; the verdicts do not depend on it: B2's turnover clause fails in all 12 pairs and B3's in all three scorers.
